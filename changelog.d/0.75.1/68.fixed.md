- **SFT runs with the rewind recorder (`rewind_log`, on by default) shuffle from the seed again
  (#68).** The recorder wrapped the train sampler, so accelerate skipped its seeded
  `SeedableRandomSampler` and the run drew its data order from the global RNG: `data_seed` had
  no effect, and the order changed with unrelated RNG use before training (gradient
  accumulation and `training.l2l` saw the same rows in different orders). The wrapper now
  applies accelerate's swap itself.

- **`training.l2l` runs in fp16, so it works on pre-Ampere cards such as the T4 (#65).** The
  L2L step backpropagates each micro-batch's head loss as `accelerator.scaler.scale(loss)`;
  the scale reaches every layer through the recompute, and unscaling, clipping and the
  inf/NaN skip stay with accelerate's optimizer step. A CPU test checks that with a scale of
  1024 the L2L LoRA gradients equal gradient accumulation's times 1024 bit for bit. fp16
  agreement with accumulation on a GPU has not been measured yet (A3-07 is calibrated for
  bf16).

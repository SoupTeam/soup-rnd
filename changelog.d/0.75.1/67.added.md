- **`training.fp16_init_scale` sets where the fp16 GradScaler starts (#67).** torch starts it
  at 65536 and skips every step that overflows while it halves; Qwen3.5 / Qwen3.8 on a T4
  overflow in the first Gated DeltaNet layer from 1024, so a run skipped its first ~7 optimizer
  steps. A power of two from 1 to 65536; transformers SFT only. On a Kaggle T4 (Qwen3.5-4B NF4
  streamed, 20 steps) the default skipped 6 steps and `256` skipped none, at the same speed
  and memory.

- **`training.l2l` runs `deepseek_v3` with `stream_expert_layout: per_expert`, and two opt-in fields `training.l2l_activation_codec` and `training.l2l_auto_k` exist (#58).** The launch admits a native DeepSeek-V3 for `task: sft` with `stream_layers: true`, `stream_source: disk` and `quantization: 4bit`; other architectures are still refused. `l2l_activation_codec` (`none` or `fp8_e4m3`, default `none`) and `l2l_auto_k` (default `false`) are refused without `l2l: true` and off `task: sft`. The schema accepts `fp8_e4m3` and `l2l_auto_k: true`, but the launch stops with a named error until the FP8 activation store and the memory planner exist, so the defaults change nothing. An SFT L2L run writes its effective k and codec to `<output>/l2l_recipe.json` before training, hands that one k to the HF arguments, the C3 reservation and the log (`l2l_effective_k`), and refuses to resume a checkpoint with another effective k or codec. On the tiny DeepSeek fixture L2L matches plain accumulation at k = 1, 4, 16 and 64 on the CPU, and the expert-read diagnostic in `docs/expert-forward.md` shows each expert is read again for every micro-batch that hits it (fetches = 3 x hits at every k), so a one-read-per-window gain is not available for experts yet. No speed claim.

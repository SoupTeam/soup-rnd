The verdict covers only the RTX 5070 Ti 16 GB set measured on a vast local volume, where the required `foreign_reads` validity check answers (50 arms `ok`, 2 `void` excluded, repeats rule met); the 3090, 3070 Ti and 3070 runs had it `unknown` and give no verdict.

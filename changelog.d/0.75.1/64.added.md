- **`training.l2l` admits the hybrid `qwen3_5` decoder (Qwen3.5 / Qwen3.8) (#64).** Its text
  model interleaves Gated DeltaNet and gated attention layers 3:1 and builds a mask per layer
  type; the L2L step now captures and replays the layer arguments per type
  (`LayerCall.for_layer`) instead of replaying layer 0's to every layer. On CPU a tiny streamed
  qwen3_5 gives L2L gradients and losses equal to gradient accumulation bit for bit at k = 1,
  2, 3. Not yet measured on a GPU.

- The layer-streaming sharder now decodes block-scaled FP8 source checkpoints (DeepSeek-V3 / Kimi K2 layout: e4m3 weights with a per-128x128 `weight_scale_inv`) before NF4, instead of casting the raw e4m3 bytes and copying the scales into the shard, which produced wrong weights without a warning.

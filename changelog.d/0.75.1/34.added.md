- **`training.l2l` scales the loss over the whole window and handles a short last window (#34).**
  L2L divides the summed loss of every micro-batch by the Hugging Face `num_items_in_batch`
  of the window, so masked labels and rows of different lengths weigh as in gradient
  accumulation. A dataset that does not divide by k ends with a shorter window that is a
  normal optimizer step; step count, `l2l_steps`, learning rate and the logged loss equal
  accumulation, also after resume. On Mistral-7B NF4 (RTX 3060, k = 4, synthetic rows) L2L
  and accumulation differ by 1.6% to 5.3% in LoRA gradients in bf16, the same order as
  switching the SDPA backend, and by 8e-6 or less in fp32. They are not bit-identical in
  bf16. `docs/performance-and-quantization.md` now lists the RAM needed for the 7B stand and
  the conditions for equal bits. Configs with `l2l: false` are unchanged.

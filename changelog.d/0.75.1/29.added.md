- **`training.l2l` loads each streamed layer once per optimizer step (#29).** With `stream_layers: true` on `task: sft`, `l2l: true` runs the k micro-batches of a gradient-accumulation window layer by layer, then takes one optimizer step on the LoRA adapters; k is `gradient_accumulation_steps`. On SmolLM2-135M (RTX 3060, k = 1, 2, 4, `none` and `4bit`) the losses and LoRA gradients matched gradient accumulation bit for bit, and layer loads stayed at 58 where accumulation needed 58, 115 and 229. The log gains `l2l_steps`, `l2l_layer_loads`, `l2l_large_loads` and `l2l_weight_restores`. Beta: it needs `lora.dropout: 0`, a llama or mistral model with `attention_dropout: 0`, one GPU and bf16 or fp32, and is refused with a message otherwise. Default `false` changes nothing. See `docs/performance-and-quantization.md`.

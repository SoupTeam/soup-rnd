- **Batch-invariant GEMM for streamed routed experts (#73).** EXPERIMENTAL
  `training.stream_expert_gemm: batch_invariant` (default `native`) runs the routed experts'
  matmuls, their input gradients and the routing-weight gradient on fixed-config Triton
  kernels whose rows do not depend on how many rows share a call. With it, route-preserving
  MBR gives the same losses and LoRA gradients as per-micro-batch two-read L2L, bit for bit,
  at any `l2l_mbr_group_size` (measured on an RTX 4060 Ti, fp32 and bf16). Requires
  `stream_expert_layout: per_expert` and CUDA; bf16 needs compute capability 8.0+. Bits are
  identical per GPU model and Triton version, with deterministic surrounding kernels and no
  deferred decoder hooks. The choice is saved in `l2l_recipe.json`; a resume with another
  value is refused.

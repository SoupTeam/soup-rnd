- **Layer streaming accepts DeepSeek-V3 (`model_type=deepseek_v3`) (#6).** The sharder stacks per-expert checkpoint keys into the fused experts transformers 5.19 builds, drops the MTP layer, and the runtime loads the router bias from the checkpoint and keeps it in fp32. A tiny synthetic model streams bit-exact against a resident one on CPU and on an RTX 3050 in fp32, for forward, backward, NF4, the disk tier, LoRA on attention and `moe_lora` on the routed experts; NF4 leaves the routed experts at the base dtype. `trust_remote_code: true` is refused for DeepSeek-V3, and `kimi_k2`, `deepseek_v32`, `deepseek_v2` and `kimi_k25` stay refused. No real DeepSeek-V3 checkpoint has been streamed. The compatibility table and raw logs are in `benchmarks/gate-b2-deepseek-v3.md`.

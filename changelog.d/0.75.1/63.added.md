- **Streamed SFT can compute the loss without the full logits (#63).** New
  `training.stream_chunked_ce` runs the causal-LM loss through `lm_head` 256 positions at a
  time and recomputes each chunk's logits in backward; `training.use_cut_ce` now works under
  `stream_layers` (it was silently ignored there) through `cut_cross_entropy` on the streamed
  head. The VRAM pre-flight and `stream_vram_probe` use the selected loss. On a Kaggle T4,
  Qwen3.5-4B streamed peaks at 3.16 GiB allocated instead of 7.34 GiB at about 9% lower
  throughput, and Qwen3.8-27B (disk tier) trains under an 8 GiB cap at 57 tok/s, where the
  transformers loss is refused. CCE does not run on sm75 (shared memory); both settings
  are SFT-only and not yet wired into `training.l2l`.

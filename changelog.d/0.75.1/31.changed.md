- **`training.l2l` accepts `lora.dropout` above 0 and equals plain gradient accumulation (#31).**
  The L2L step records which dropouts a layer call draws, replays them in accumulation
  order to get the generator state before each (micro-batch, layer) pair, runs the forward
  and the recompute at that state, and leaves the outer stream where accumulation would.
  With dropout above 0 it matches an unmodified Hugging Face Trainer. Refused: `neftune_alpha`
  together with dropout, `attention_dropout` above 0 with `sdpa` attention (use
  `attn_implementation='eager'`), and a window of rows of different lengths together with
  dropout. Checked on CPU and on an RTX 3060 with SmolLM2-135M. Configs with `l2l: false` are unchanged.

## Dense shaped-block timing: NO VERDICT

The fixture uses hidden=4096, KV=1024, intermediate=14336, rank=16, batch=1,
128 tokens. It is an 8B-shaped projection/block fixture, not a complete 8B model.
The corresponding dense correctness gate passes before timing. Forward and
backward use separate synchronized CUDA events, 5 warmups and 10 ABBA rounds,
with two samples per implementation per round; 240 raw arms per dtype.

Offline telemetry review excludes entire rounds with unequal recorded SM clocks
or power states: 1 BF16 round and 2 FP16 rounds. Sampled telemetry is finite and
records only the benchmark's own visible GPU process for included rounds.
All excluded measurements remain in the raw artifacts.

Warmup, inference and optimizer/full-training costs are outside the timed region.
These limitations keep the performance verdict **NO VERDICT**. The measurements
show no consistent improvement and include slower cases. NF4 performance and
Liger/Unsloth comparison were not measured.

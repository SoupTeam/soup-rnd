E2 hybrid across seeds and smaller K. Quality check on Kaggle.
Prospective protocol. Committed before the first arm of this series runs. After that,
nothing here changes; corrections go into a dated ADDENDUM file next to this one.

1. Question

The hybrid schedule (LoRA on all 32 layers in epoch 1, bottom LoRA frozen after step
2,000, top K only in epoch 2) kept all-layer quality at K=24 in one seed and missed the
MMLU-mini margin by two questions at K=16 (benchmarks/e2/hybrid/reports/20261105).
The speed the activation cache can give grows as K shrinks. This series asks, over three
seeds: at which of K = 24, 16, 8 does the hybrid stay within the E2 quality margin?

Quality only; the cache is off; no speed is claimed (a speed check with the cache on is a
separate, later protocol, run only for a K that passes here).

2. Platform and code

As benchmarks/e2/hybrid/PROTOCOL.txt section 2 (Kaggle GPU T4 x2, Mistral-7B-v0.3 at
caa1feb0e54d415e2df31207e5f4e273e33509b1, float16 base, kit verified by the notebook).
Library versions (torch, transformers, peft) must equal those of the 20261105 hybrid
session (torch 2.11.0+cu130, transformers 5.20.2, peft 0.21.2), since its seed-42 arms
are reused; otherwise NO_VERDICT.

Runner: benchmarks/e2/hybrid/run_hybrid.py, extended with --seed (default 42, the
previous behaviour) and with --keep-top optional (absent = no freeze: an all-layer run).
The seed sets Python, NumPy and torch seeds in the runner and the config's training.seed
and data_seed (LoRA initialisation and training order). The 200 validation rows are the
positional tail of the data file and do not depend on the seed.

3. Arms

Seeds 901 and 1234, each: W32 (all layers), H24, H16, H8 (hybrid, keep top K).
Seed 42: H8 (new); W32, H24, H16 reused from hybrid/reports/20261105.
Seed 42 anchor: ANCHOR_s42 = W32 through the extended runner with --seed 42 and no
--keep-top, training only (no gate). Its validation CE must equal the 20261105 W32 CE
(0.8757693767547607) to 1e-9 relative: this shows the extended runner reproduces the
original one bit for bit.

All arms use recipe_check/configs/W32.yaml with base, output, seed and data_seed
replaced. Gate suites as before (eight suites, scorer revision 5, re-scored offline).

Sessions (any order, one attempt each): ["W32_s901", "H24_s901", "H16_s901", "H8_s901"],
["W32_s1234", "H24_s1234", "H16_s1234", "H8_s1234"], ["ANCHOR_s42", "H8_s42"].

4. Validity (any failure gives NO_VERDICT for the series, with the reason)

  V1  every arm complete as in recipe_check PROTOCOL.txt V1.
  V2  ANCHOR_s42 CE equals the 20261105 W32 CE to 1e-9 relative.
  V3  hybrid mechanics in every H arm as in hybrid PROTOCOL.txt V3, with the frozen
      layers 0 .. 31-K.
  V4  every config equals the W32 template apart from base, output, seed and data_seed,
      and seed/data_seed equal the arm's seed.
  V5  library versions as in section 2.

5. Decision, per K in {24, 16, 8}, over the three seeds

  gap_s = 100 * (CE(H_K, s) - CE(W32, s)) / CE(W32, s)          (same seed)
  delta_s(suite) = score(H_K, s) - score(W32, s), for mini_mmlu, mini_common_sense,
                   mini_instruction, mini_arithmetic, mini_tool_call, mini_format_json
  mean over seeds: mean_gap, mean_delta(suite)

  WITHIN_MARGIN  mean_gap <= 0.5, every gap_s <= 1.0, every mean_delta >= -0.05,
                 and mini_over_refusal(H_K, s) >= 0.95 in every seed
  BORDERLINE     not WITHIN_MARGIN, mean_gap <= 1.0, every mean_delta >= -0.05,
                 and over-refusal holds in every seed
  FAILS          otherwise

Averaging the suite deltas over three seeds is the change from the one-seed rule: one
seed's suites have 24-40 items, where one question is 0.025-0.04. The smallest K labelled
WITHIN_MARGIN is the K this series recommends for the speed check; none if no K passes.

6. Stopping

As recipe_check PROTOCOL.txt section 7: one attempt per arm, one restart only for a
failure before training starts (reason written into the results), 5 hours per arm.
Kaggle's free quota only.

7. Never claimed

A measured speed-up; cache parity across the freeze; models, data, recipes or epoch
counts other than these.

Compare baseline, coherence_filter, length_curriculum and reverse_length_curriculum
at exactly 5,000,000 shifted nonignored useful training targets each. Use one
fixed Mistral-7B-v0.3 revision, tokenizer, LoRA recipe, runtime, seed901 and the
same frozen held-out 256 examples / 322,332 evaluation targets. Each arm starts
with the original model, fresh adapter and fresh optimizer. Exactly 611 optimizer
updates: 610 of 8,192 targets and a last update of 2,880. The LR horizon stays 5M.
These are the existing engine and prepared input plans, not a new data policy.

Operational limits and authority
An arm has a hard 14,400-second supervisor limit plus 30 seconds before TERM and
10 seconds before KILL; internal stage guards remain active. Four hours is an
operational ceiling, not a guaranteed runtime prediction. Pilot linear token,
processed-slot and squared-length extrapolations are only train-time heuristics;
short ordered prefixes differ from the full length distribution. In particular,
attention-proxy extrapolation for the short-first pilot is highly uncertain.
The original model's longest prepared sequences must fit; a pilot does not prove
that every full step will succeed. Preserve all failed/incomplete evidence.

The user explicitly waived financial-reserve limits. This entry point requires

Evaluation and deliverable
Primary metric: token-weighted held-out mean CE, on identical target counts.
Report CE versus baseline, useful/nonpadding/processed/padded token counts,
training-phase seconds, useful-target throughput and peak allocated/reserved VRAM.
The original frozen diagnostic suite is secondary; generation, scoring and
128-token limit are unchanged. Export blinded forms with the unchanged summarize
helper. Independent safety review remains pending; quality remains NO_VERDICT
until reviewed. One seed supports exploratory comparisons, not a statistically
established winner. Filtering effects concern that policy's full composition;
never attribute a combined change to a single mechanism without an ablation.
Training-phase timing excludes preparation, model setup, evaluation and reload.
Do not label it complete end-to-end acceleration or include interrupted pilot
whole-run timing. Preparation/filtering cost is not retrospectively measured.
Guest controls do not certify unobservable physical-host isolation. No release
speedup, safety acceptance or broader generalization claim follows automatically.

Commands
Modes: check (offline, no GPU), full (one new bounded arm), review (all four full
roots plus blinded exports). Common options: --spec --spec-sha --reference
--verification --series --pilots --artifact-root --data --cuda-gate --out.
The first three evidence roots are original baseline, its separate reload check
and original continuation series; --pilots is the pinned three completed pilots.
Full also requires --arm --model-dir --launch-config --launch-config-sha
--admission --protocol-commit. Review requires --runs ROOT1 ROOT2 ROOT3 ROOT4.
Use the external supervisor, not its hidden worker; preserve and audit each arm
before the next. A source commit string alone is not proof of pushed/deployed
bytes; the operator verifies those independently before any paid full run.

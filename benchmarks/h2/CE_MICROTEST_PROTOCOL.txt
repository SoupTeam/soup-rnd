H2 CE-function micro-test protocol (prospective; attempt017)
===========================================================
Date: 2026-10-29. Script: benchmarks/harness/h2_ce_microtest.py. Plan: attempt017_plan.md revision 2e (logits drawn on the CPU as in revision 2c).
Plan revision 2e. Fixed before any run. The regimes, constants and interpretation below were written AFTER attempt015 and
attempt016 were seen by everyone involved, so they are NOT blind to those results.

What this is and is not
-----------------------
An EXPLANATORY micro-test of the cross-entropy function alone. It asks whether Liger's fp32 CE gradient is
further from an fp64 reference than PyTorch's on synthetic bf16 logits, and how many bf16-propagated gradient
elements differ. It cannot turn the attempt015/016 BLOCKED outcomes into PASS. numerical_policy.json is not
read, used or changed; the CE runner, workset, fixture and every pinned runner file are untouched (the script
only READS ce_confirmation_workset.json for shapes and masks). It is not H2 acceptance and not a speed result.

CONVENTIONS (arbitrary, decisive, not derived, not tuned to the 015/016 numbers)
  u = 2^-24 (fp32 unit roundoff; not arbitrary); A = 17 u (17 = ceil(log2 128256), an ASSUMPTION that an
  fp32 reduction behaves like a tree of that depth); Rho = 2; the 50% threshold of label (a); the 10% marginal
  band; the gate rule in section 5; the logit scales; five seeds; 200 cases. They are fixed when this file is
  committed.

Design
------
One fresh process, two phases. Phase 1 runs every PyTorch case (including repeats) with the stock
F.cross_entropy. Then ONE rebind through the real
apply_liger_kernel_to_llama(rope=False, cross_entropy=True, fused_linear_cross_entropy=False,
rms_norm=False, swiglu=False), which is never restored, followed by a delegating call counter. Phase 2 runs
every Liger case. The binding is asserted before every case.
Per case: bf16 logits z = bf16(s * randn(N, V)), i.i.d. Gaussian with standard deviation s, drawn on the CPU
from a seeded generator, rounded to bf16 on the CPU and copied to the GPU (no CUDA matmul is involved, so
regeneration in phase 2 cannot depend on the cuBLAS algorithm chosen), V = 128256; labels from the real frozen probe rows (shapes [1,24],
[1,48], [1,96], [2,192]; 6, 12, 24, 54 targets) or, in the self-consistent regimes, targets drawn from the
softmax (only the ignore mask and shapes are then real). Regimes: random targets at s = 1, 2, 3 and
self-consistent targets at s = 1.5, 2; 5 seeds (0..4); paths 'mean' (probe path) and 'sum24' (sum divided by
num_items_in_batch = 24, the real training-window denominator at every shape). 4 x 5 x 5 x 2 = 200 cases.
Implementations: P = transformers ForCausalLMLoss with stock cross_entropy; L = the same call after the
rebind; R = fp64 reference from the SAME fp32 values, computed two independent ways (shifted softmax and
log-sum-exp) that must agree to 1e-13 relative, else VOID; alt = an independent PyTorch fp32 log-sum-exp route
with autograd (empirical anchor). Each of P and L is evaluated for two variants (fp32 leaf = the fp32-level
gradient; bf16 leaf = the gradient that really propagates) and twice each (determinism repeats; non-equal
repeats are REPORTED, never drop a case).
In-place hazard: Liger writes its gradient over its input. The master tensors are never passed to either
implementation; every call gets a fresh clone; after each case the master logits hash is re-checked
(mismatch = VOID); P modifying its clone is VOID; L modifying its clone is only recorded.
Same-input guarantee: phase 1 stores SHA-256 of the logits and labels of every case and the P fp32 gradients
on disk; phase 2 regenerates each case from its seed and verifies both hashes (mismatch = VOID).

Metrics
-------
Per case, fp32 level, against R: E = global relative L2, M = max-abs / max|R|, C = cosine; rho = E_L /
max(E_P, u). D = relative L2 between implementations ((P,L), (P,alt), (L,alt)). bf16 level (PRIMARY
descriptive outcome, no pass/fail label): count of bf16 elements with bf16(g_X) != bf16(R) for X in {P, L},
the P-vs-L flip count, and the downstream dh = dlogits_bf16 @ W relative L2 between P and L and each against
dh from bf16(R) (W: a synthetic bf16 [V, 4096] matrix drawn once on the CPU from its own seed 20261029 and
copied to the GPU, 1.05 GB; one matmul, not the 32-layer amplification).

Run status (evaluated first)
----------------------------
VOID if any of: pin mismatch (torch 2.14.1, liger-kernel 0.8.5, transformers 5.17.0, triton 3.8.0), a
different GPU class than the contract, a foreign GPU process at start, F.cross_entropy binding wrong before
the rebind or before any case, the Liger function not bound, a failed kernel-ran check (section 6), a phase-1 /
phase-2 input hash mismatch, a non-finite value, a reference self-check above 1e-13, a master/clone integrity
failure, a delegate call count other than one per loss evaluation, or any unexpected failure. INCOMPLETE if
fewer than all 200 cases ran (a missing case is never dropped silently). Otherwise COMPLETE.

CE-level label for a COMPLETE run (first match wins)
----------------------------------------------------
a. BOTH_OUTSIDE_A_AT_CE_LEVEL_SYNTHETIC: in at least half of the 200 cases both E_P > A and E_L > A.
b. LIGER_WORSE_AT_CE_LEVEL_SYNTHETIC: not (a) and at least one case has E_L > A and rho > Rho.
c. LIGER_NOT_WORSE_AT_CE_LEVEL_SYNTHETIC: not (a) or (b), and in every case E_L <= A and rho <= Rho.
d. INCONCLUSIVE_AT_CE_LEVEL: everything else (e.g. E_L <= A everywhere but rho > Rho somewhere: a floor effect).
MARGINAL is reported when a deciding quantity is within 10% of A or Rho (for (a): the case count within 10% of
half the cases).

Representativeness gate (loss-paired)
-------------------------------------
HIGH-loss cell: random-target s = 2 at the 24- and 48-row shapes, both paths, all 5 seeds: the P-vs-L bf16
flip count must be ZERO in every case. LOW-loss cell: self-consistent s = 1.5 at the 96- and 384-position
shapes, per shape and path: at least one flip in at least 4 of 5 seeds. The other cells are descriptive. If
the gate fails the run is labelled NOT_REPRESENTATIVE: the CE-level label is still reported, the explanatory
reading is WITHHELD.

Kernel-ran rule (one rule)
--------------------------
(a) Every Liger case runs through a delegating call counter on the rebound function (binding checked against
wrapper.__wrapped__); a count other than one call per loss evaluation (4 per case) is VOID. (b) A profiler
capture on a fixed subset: the first case (seed 0, first regime) of each of the 4 shapes on both paths (8
Liger cases) and, on the PyTorch side, the first case of the 24-row and the 384-position shapes on the 'mean'
path (2 cases). A kernel event whose name contains liger_cross_entropy must appear in every captured Liger
case and in none of the captured PyTorch cases; any miss is VOID.

Receipts
--------
At start and end: versions, GPU name/UUID/driver, nvidia-smi clocks and throttle reasons, GPU process list,
boot ID, deterministic and TF32 flags. Output: result.json (per-case records, label, gate, anchor, bf16
primary outcome, profiler checks, receipts, seconds). The script's sha256 is pinned in the attempt contract.

What may be said
----------------
"CE-function accuracy only, synthetic logits. This says nothing about which LoRA-B gradient is nearer the
truth or why probes 02 and 03 differ. The attempt015/016 BLOCKED outcomes and the policy are unchanged. fp32
accuracy parity does not imply the same bf16-propagated gradients. Explanatory; it cannot turn attempt015/016
into PASS." The words "equally valid implementations" or any exoneration language are not allowed.

Limitations (realism caveats)
-----------------------------
The logits are not from a CUDA matmul or a model, so bf16 rounding differs slightly from the real lm_head
path. Entries are independent across the vocabulary (a real lm_head output has structure and heavy tails),
independent of the labels, and not low rank; the construction reproduces bf16 rounding of logits and the
shape/dtype path, not the real logit distribution. Also: rho is dominated by the u floor when
PyTorch is near-exact; the self-consistent regime does not use the real labels; GPU-class specific (RTX 4090
class). The phase-1 / phase-2 SHA-256 check of logits and labels stays; a mismatch is VOID, not silently
compared. CPU cost: up to 384 x 128256 normals per case (about 49 M numbers), twice per case (UNVERIFIED,
expected seconds). CPU tests cover the logic with a stand-in Liger module and
cannot exercise the Triton kernel, the CUDA matmul or the profiler.

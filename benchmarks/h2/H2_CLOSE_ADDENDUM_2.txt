H2-close protocol, addendum 2 (2026-11-04). Review-code correction and re-audit of retained
evidence after the review of 2026-11-04. No GPU run, rental, threshold, rule, workload, arm or
label changes. H2_CLOSE_PROTOCOL.txt and addendum 1 are unchanged.

1. Defect
U2 requires every tensor of the declared trainable manifest to change its bytes. The stage U
review (h2_close_updates.review_updates) relied on h2_ce_training.read_training, which rejects
a step only when no adapter tensor changed. A declared tensor that never changed could therefore
pass. The reviewer showed this with a CPU fixture; the same negative control (one declared tensor
joined to the loss through a zero-valued term) gives UPDATES_PASS on the measured code.

2. Correction (review code only)
review_updates now fails an arm with row U2 when any declared trainable tensor has the same
final and initial SHA256 (h2_close_updates.unchanged_adapters), and reports changed/declared.
read_training has already tied those digests to the stored tensor bytes. The negative control
is a regression test. The measured code that produced the published results is unchanged in its
archive and is identified by its contract SHA256; the corrected reviewer is newer by design.

Archive 7c20a5ff1d20eca121a036a669e9617d0f3f39ea51d3609198313c3eb551205e (instance 58810258),
reassembled and checked against its receipt and all 163 member SHA256 of its manifest.
h2_close_reaudit.py updates: A and L U2_REAUDIT_PASS, 448 of 448 declared adapter tensors

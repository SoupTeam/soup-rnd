H2 CE controls addendum — 2026-10-28
Prospective addition to CE_CONFIRMATION_PROTOCOL.txt. No prior evidence is rewritten.

Each of the four fresh numerical processes now captures its own controls.
Before the first CUDA allocation, nvidia-smi must report no compute processes.
A one-element allocation and synchronization on the sole visible GPU establish
its host PID using the unchanged exclusive-local-CUDA-initialization method in
l2l_box.py. Never infer ownership from a previously observed PID or alias it to
process I/O counters. Every sampled GPU PID list must contain exactly that PID.
Sample at initialization, every 2 seconds, and at completion. Unknown readings,
foreign processes, missing samples or missing ownership block acceptance.

Use unchanged ArmWatch with all three F1 checks required: suspend, power and
foreign_reads, on the pinned model snapshot's physical disk. Preserve existing
1,000,000,000-byte foreign-read threshold; do not override sensors or required
checks. Capture begins before model loading and ends after numerical capture.
Every F1 check must be ok. Unknown/void is rejected. Retain clocks and event
reasons; require complete finite positive clocks. Throttling is recorded without
turning it into a numerical failure or claiming throughput acceptance.

Stop samplers before evidence sealing. Retain controls.json on failures. A failed
control changes the child to FAILED and blocks later phases without retries.
The offline reader recomputes controls acceptance in addition to tensor/hash
checks. Include this addendum, h2_ce_controls.py, arm_validity.py and l2l_box.py in
the committed artifact pin set. Original numeric thresholds, workload and
shared2400s/600s child/40s cleanup deadlines remain unchanged. The outer watchdog
continues to bound a stalled sensor; no control can extend the shared deadline.

Still required operationally: preflight source/boot/GPU/runtime, bounded setup,
cumulative budget, verified physical snapshot placement, complete local export
and SHA audit before resource deletion. Local fault tests do not establish live
sensor availability or GPU correctness. NO_VERDICT until the relevant GPU evidence.

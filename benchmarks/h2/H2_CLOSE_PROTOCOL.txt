H2-close protocol: actual updates, Unsloth and interleaved throughput (prospective)
Written 2026-11-02, before any stage U, S or P arm. Commit it before the first arm and do not edit
it afterwards; each attempt contract pins its sha256. A later change is a new, dated addendum that
applies only to attempts started after it is committed.

0. Purpose and what does not change
Close the four open items of the H2 card: actual adapter updates (U), Unsloth on the same recipe
(S), measured full-training throughput and peak VRAM in interleaved runs (P), and a multiplier
published only for the measured configuration.
Unchanged and not reinterpreted by this protocol: NUMERICAL_POLICY.txt/numerical_policy.json and
their thresholds; attempts 015 and 016 stay BLOCKED; attempt019 stays NOT_CALIBRATABLE; every
earlier NO_VERDICT stays. No stage below turns a gradient difference into a pass or a fail of the
old policy. Performance claims come only from stage P.

1. Arms
A  baseline: stock Transformers/TRL SFT, candidate_configs/baseline.json (U) and
   perf_baseline.json (P).
L  Liger CE-only: the same recipe with only the cross-entropy rebound to liger_cross_entropy
   through the existing PatchController (h2_operator_patch.py), as in attempts 015-019. No
   RMSNorm, RoPE, SwiGLU or fused-linear-CE kernel may be active; attempt013 showed those break
   the gradient thresholds and they are out of scope. Configs liger.json / perf_liger_ce.json.
U  Unsloth: backend unsloth, unsloth/unsloth-zoo 2026.10.7 from runtime_candidate.lock, configs
   unsloth.json / perf_unsloth.json. Unsloth chooses its own kernels, dtype handling and
   checkpointing; flags alone are not equivalence. Its actual choices are recorded, not assumed.
All arms: unsloth/Meta-Llama-3.1-8B revision e9a141a2091ea561b96483212645a2a05e6f99fc from the
verified 11-file snapshot, NF4 QLoRA r16/alpha32/dropout0 on seven projections, AdamW 2e-4
constant, batch 1, accumulation 4, seed and data_seed 42, gradient checkpointing, llama3 template,
responses-only loss. Only the backend/CE binding and output paths differ.

2. Stage U: actual updates, arms A and L (one fresh process each)
Workload: the frozen training batches of ce_confirmation_workset.json (16 microbatches drawn
from prepared_fixture_candidate.jsonl, 4 optimizer updates, 96 useful targets), run by the
existing, already tested training phase of h2_ce_training.py. That phase never ran on a GPU:
attempts 015/016 stopped at the probe comparison, which is not a gate here.
Gate rows, each per arm; any failure gives UPDATES_FAIL with the failing row:
U1 exactly 16 microbatches and 4 optimizer updates in the frozen order; every accumulation
   window complete; num_items_in_batch is the Python integer 24 in every window (hardening R2).
U2 h2_update_oracle.py: every tensor of the exact expected trainable manifest changed its bytes;
   no frozen parameter, buffer or NF4 quant-state byte changed (h2_quant_state.py).
U3 every logged loss and every gradient norm finite; no gradient norm exactly 0.0.
U4 work equivalence (h2_work_equivalence.py): ordered input ids, masks, labels and counts equal
   between A and L and equal to the frozen workset.
U5 attribution: L shows liger_cross_entropy_kernel launches and no other Liger kernel; A shows
   no Liger kernel; in L all 16 gradient-enabled training CE calls are bound to the Liger CE
   (reduction sum, ignore_index -100), in A none.
Validity (void, not fail): the F1 checks of section 5 and GPU exclusivity on each process.
Recorded, never gated: per-step loss difference L-A, final adapter relative L2 and cosine L vs A
per tensor, optimizer-state difference. These are measurements; the old policy is not applied.
Outcome per arm: UPDATES_PASS, UPDATES_FAIL(row) or VOID(check).

3. Stage S: Unsloth, arm U (one fresh audited process)
Stage U's harness is built around the Transformers/PatchController path, so arm U is audited on
the stage P path instead: `h2_perf_abba.py audit U`, the perf workload, 20 optimizer steps,
the same trainer path as a stage P run plus update evidence. Unsloth is imported first in the
process, before torch and transformers. An audited run hashes the whole model and is never timed.
S1 the pinned stack must import, load the 8B snapshot in 4-bit and train. An import, load or
   kernel-compilation error, or a refusal by Unsloth of the pinned Transformers/TRL versions,
   gives UNSLOTH_INCOMPATIBLE with the full error. No other Unsloth or library version is tried
   under this protocol.
S2 work: 20 optimizer steps measured; per-step useful and total token counts observed in
   training_step equal the frozen 20-step sequence of perf_workload_receipt.json.
S3 updates (h2_update_oracle.audit_update): adapter bytes changed; no frozen parameter, buffer
   or NF4 quant-state byte changed; finite nonzero adapter gradients observed before optimizer
   steps; every loss finite; bench validity (no zero or non-finite gradient norm). The trainable
   manifest must hold LoRA A and B on all seven target projections in every layer.
S4 record, never gated: Unsloth version; parameter and trainable dtypes; gradient-checkpointing
   flag; forward bindings of model, attention, MLP, norms and the loss function; per-step
   losses. No kernel profile is taken.
Declared differences that are part of arm U and are not equalized: Unsloth builds its own
4-bit config (double quantization on, as in A); Unsloth's get_peft_model initializes the LoRA
weights with its own random state, not seed 42, so initial adapters differ from A; Soup asks
for use_gradient_checkpointing="unsloth"; dtype is chosen by Unsloth. soup bench train refuses
the Unsloth backend by name; the runner skips that scope check for arm U only, and S2/S3 stand
in for it.
Outcome: UPDATES_PASS, UPDATES_FAIL(row), UNSLOTH_INCOMPATIBLE or VOID(check).

4. Stage P: interleaved throughput and peak VRAM
P1 Workload. benchmarks/h2/perf_workload.jsonl, 96 synthetic English-like rows, written by
   benchmarks/harness/h2_perf_workload.py (seed 20261102). Real token accounting is frozen in
   perf_workload_receipt.json by h2_perf_receipt.py: 404-956 tokens per formatted row, mean
   686.35, 65,890 tokens, 47,981 useful targets (72.82%), no row reaches max_length 1024, and A
   and L prepare identical ids and targets. Systems workload only: no quality claim.
P2 Arms admitted. A always; L only with UPDATES_PASS in stage U; U only with UPDATES_PASS in
   stage S. An arm not admitted is reported as not measured, with the stage outcome.
P3 Run. One run = one fresh process on one arm: 20 optimizer steps, the first 4 discarded as
   warm-up, 16 counted (64 rows). Same row order in every arm (data_seed 42).
   The run number is useful tokens per second over the 16 counted steps (useful target tokens /
   summed counted step wall time, CUDA-synchronized step boundaries, as in soup bench train).
   Deviation from METHOD.md, declared here: METHOD's run number is the median step time; rows
   here have different lengths by design, so the token-weighted rate is used. Median step time,
   total tokens/s and every step time are recorded too.
   Peak VRAM per run: torch.cuda.max_memory_allocated and max_memory_reserved, reset at the
   start of training (soup bench train).
P4 Run validity. A run is VALID only if: the child exits 0 within 600 s; 20 steps measured;
   the bench validity checks pass (trainable parameters present, parameters changed, no zero or
   non-finite gradient norm); the useful and total token counts of all 20 steps equal the frozen
   step sequence in perf_workload_receipt.json (one real 20-step CPU run of arm A through the
   same runner, tiny stand-in model); for L, PatchController.verify passes after training; the
   F1 checks of section 5 are not void; no other process on the GPU before or after.
   A void F1 check or a non-exclusive GPU voids the run and its block; the block is replaced
   (METHOD), all records kept. Any other invalid run is a code or setup failure that a
   replacement would only repeat: the attempt stops at once with FAILED_RUN.
P5 Block. Three arms: A L U U L A. Two arms: A X X A. Linear drift cancels for every pair inside
   a block. Each pair (A,L) and (A,U) gets one difference per block: mean of its arm's two runs
   minus mean of A's two runs.
P6 Repeats (METHOD.md, per pair). 5 pilot blocks, counted. sigma = pooled run-to-run SD of the
   pair, delta = 10% of the mean of A, N = max(2, ceil(7.85 * (sigma/delta)^2)). Run blocks up
   to the largest N over admitted pairs, recompute N once from all blocks, top up once, stop.
   Each pair's test uses only its own first N valid blocks, so blocks run for the other pair
   cannot move it.
   Hard cap: 12 valid blocks per stage P attempt (budget). A pair whose N exceeds 12 gets
   NO_VERDICT_INSUFFICIENT_REPETITIONS. More than 4 void blocks in one attempt stops it:
   NO_VERDICT_HOST_NOISE. No new block starts when the remaining lease cannot hold one:
   INCOMPLETE_DEADLINE, and pairs short of N get NO_VERDICT_INSUFFICIENT_REPETITIONS.
P7 Test and labels, per pair, on the valid blocks: mean block difference d, two-sided one-sample
   t-test against 0, alpha 0.05, 95% t interval. Ratio r = 1 + d / mean(A useful tok/s), with
   the interval scaled the same way.
   FASTER if the interval lies above 0; SLOWER if below 0; NO_DETECTABLE_DIFFERENCE otherwise,
   reported with its interval (it is not equivalence).
P8 Peak VRAM per arm: median over its valid runs of each measure; difference to A reported with
   min-max. Descriptive; no threshold.
P9 Multiplier. Published only for a pair with a label from P7, as "r (95% interval) useful
   tokens/s on <GPU, driver, workload sha, arm config sha>", plus peak VRAM. Never extrapolated
   to other GPUs, models, lengths, quantization or other Liger operators.
P10 Runner. benchmarks/harness/h2_perf_abba.py: run_arm (one run, soup bench train's trainer
   path), Series (box driver, fresh child per run inside ArmWatch) and analyze (pure; the
   offline review recomputes every label from series.json). No profiler in timed runs; L's
   kernel attribution comes from stage U's trace and the per-run patch bindings. Arm U runs
   as in stage S without the audit, Unsloth imported first.

5. Validity checks on every process (stages U, S and P)
benchmarks/harness/arm_validity.py, required checks: suspend, power, foreign_reads (model disk,
1 GB limit). GPU clock record kept for every process (never voids, always reported; any
sw_power_cap or thermal slowdown in counted windows is listed in the report). GPU exclusivity:
nvidia-smi shows no other compute process before and after each process. Box identity (boot id,
GPU UUID) constant within a lease.

6. Leases, budget and stop rules
Total budget for this protocol: 6.00 USD of displayed Vast spend. Planned split: stages U and S
on one lease, cap 1.50; stage P, cap 3.00; reserve 1.50, usable only for one repeat of stage P
after an attempt ends in NO_VERDICT_HOST_NOISE or an F1-voided series, never after a code
failure. Machine: one RTX 4090, whole machine rented (gpu_frac 1, single GPU), verified,
reliability >= 99%, total rate <= 0.60 USD/h with disk; 32 GB container plus 100 GB local
volume; template "C3 H2 existing-key owner recovery 002"; protected resources untouched.
Lease length = min(150 min, floor(cap / rate) minutes); dispatch refuses when the remaining
lease cannot cover the stage bound plus export margin.
Bounds: U and S 600 s per process, 2400 s per stage; P 600 s per run; P series bound from the
lease. External watchdog on every process. No retry, resume or partial rerun of a failed
process; a void P block is replaced only under P6.
Evidence: preserved with a manifest, exported to the USB drive, archive sha256 verified on the
Mac before the instance and its volume are destroyed; local APFS restore and an offline review
before any report. Billing and endpoint data stay out of public files.

7. Mapping to the H2 card checklist
1 same 8B recipe for every arm: section 1, stage U/S manifests, P4 token equality.
2 versions, targets, numerics, token accounting: runtime lock, configs, receipts, S3, P1.
3 actual adapter updates: stages U and S (U2).
4 full tok/s and memory, interleaved: stage P.
5 multiplier only for the measured configuration: P9.
Dependencies D1/D2/D3 and H1 are reported with their state at the time of the report; their
acceptance is the reviewer's decision and is not assumed here.

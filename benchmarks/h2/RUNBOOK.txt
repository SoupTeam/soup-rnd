Implemented correctness evidence
- h2_update_oracle.py: exact expected trainable manifest, before/after adapter
  bytes, frozen parameters/buffers, finite loss/gradients and nonzero updates.
- h2_quant_state.py: packed NF4 bytes plus reviewed Params4bit/QuantState decode
  state, nested scales/codebook/offset, shape/dtype and source identity. Unknown,
  uninitialized, nonfinite or inconsistent schemas fail. Actual tiny CPU NF4
  quantize/dequantize and hidden-state mutation controls pass; not8B QLoRA.
- h2_work_equivalence.py: ordered input IDs, masks, labels and counts, backwards
  and complete accumulation boundaries. Equal counts with different masks fail.
- h2_numerics.py: deterministic first collated row, train-mode scalar model loss
  and raw adapter gradients, no optimizer; restore torch RNG/module modes and
  clear probe gradients. Safetensors export and independent CPU metrics enforce
  the prospective NUMERICAL_POLICY.txt/JSON. Limits are not validated on8B CUDA.
  One-row model parity does not verify TRL accumulation normalization/full32-step
  numerical equivalence, model quality or kernel attribution.
- h2_trainer_smoke.py: actual offline Soup/TRL/PEFT tiny random CPU Llama. Normal
  updates pass; no-op optimizer and wrong targets fail. Per-batch/backward/step
  JSONL and profiler operation names are retained. Not throughput evidence.
- h2_gpu_correctness.py: unvalidated fresh-process prototype for exact Llama3.1
  architecture/LoRA shapes,NF4/bf16, one CUDA device, backend attribution and
  correct observed work/updates. Requires committed code, exact versions/model
  files, numerical policy SHA, live boot/GPU identity and explicit H2 authority.
  Module/forward names do not substitute for independent executed-kernel review.
- h2_watchdog.py: local POSIX process-group supervision, whole-child timeout,
  TERM/KILL with a held session leader, bounded logs and failure receipts. Real
  macOS tests cover resistant descendants, orphan cleanup, isolated process
  survival, exits and flood limits. No arbitrary-session containment or Linux
  CUDA guarantee is claimed. It cannot stop provider billing or delete resources.

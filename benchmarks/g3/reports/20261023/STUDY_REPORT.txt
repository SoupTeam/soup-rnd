What the evidence establishes
The corrected primitive can compile against a real CUDA/cuFile SDK. Ordinary
transfer controls and the independent disk sensor calibration worked on the
third attempt. The tested professional-GPU hosts were not ready for the frozen
nvidia-fs route. These observations do not show that A40/A100/L40S or Vast as a
whole cannot support GDS. P2PDMA was not enabled or tested as an alternative.
No comparative bandwidth, speed multiplier, CPU reduction, internal total
pinned-memory saving or training-throughput claim is made. Two passing single
control reads are correctness evidence, not an ABBA performance study.
